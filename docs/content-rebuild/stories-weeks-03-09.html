The Journey

Your Stories
Weeks 3 to 9

29 questions, one sitting
ANSWER ANY WAY YOU LIKE
Talk, scribble, or bullet points. Each answer drops into that day’s audio script.
Week 3

The Poser

Day 15 · Free in Theory

When's the last time you felt truly free? Nothing to hide, nothing to prove. Where were you, and what were you doing?

Day 16 · Survival Mode

Think of a stretch when work or home had you under real pressure (not the divorce season; you told that one in Week 2). What did you do to get through the day?

Day 18 · The Version They See

What's something you do around people that isn't really you? When did you do it last, how did it go, and did it cost you or anybody close to you something?

Day 19 · The Fig Leaf

Finish this sentence the way you'd say it about yourself: "I'm just the kind of guy who..." When did that line come out of your mouth last, and what was going on?

Week 4

The Wound

Day 23 · Two Questions

When's a time one of your kids (or grandkids) yelled "Watch this!" and needed you to see him? What happened, and what did his face do when you looked?

Day 24 · His Eighth Birthday

Do you have a picture of yourself as a boy, around eight? What was that kid like? What did he love? What was he scared of?

Day 26 · Every Wound Carries a Message

When's a time as a grown man that "nothing I do is good enough" was running the show? At work, at home, with your kids. What happened?

Day 27 · It Mattered

Before you could see it as a wound, how did you talk about the way you grew up? What did you tell people, or yourself?

Week 5

The New Name

Day 30 · What Is True

Is there something you used to believe about yourself that you don't believe anymore? What was it, and what changed?

Day 31 · Still Need a Father

Make your own short list: where do you still need God to father you right now? Pick one. What's going on there that you honestly don't know how to handle?

Day 33 · The Yellow Passport

Was there a season you carried something like Valjean's yellow passport, a name or label that told people who you were before you opened your mouth? How did it run your life? (If the jail story fits here and you want to use it, this is the spot. If not, any name you lived under works. You don't have to say the word itself.)

Day 35 · Not a Test

How has your own name or word from God come to you over the years? Fast or slow, clear or fuzzy, all at once or in pieces? Is there anything you're still asking Him?

Week 6

A Battle to Fight

Day 37 · Real Event, False Spin

When's a time something real happened, and right after it a voice in your head told you what it meant about you? What happened, and what exactly did that voice say?

Day 38 · Robert Sat Down

Which one gets you more, accusation or diminishment? What does it actually say in your head, word for word? When did it last show up?

Day 39 · Break It Out Loud

What's a lie you lived under for a long time? Keep what happened short. What did you start believing, what did it do to your life, and what happened when you finally stopped agreeing with it? What's that fight like now?

Day 41 · Beginning to Believe

When's a time you stopped running from something and turned around to face it? What happened? (Optional: if your jail story belongs anywhere, it may be here. Only if you want it.)

Week 7

An Adventure to Live

Day 43 · What Did You Want?

When you were a kid, what did you want to be when you grew up? When did you stop saying it out loud?

Day 44 · Bigger Than a Weekend

Leave out hunting, fishing and trips. What's an adventure you've been in where your heart was on the line with another person? What did it ask of you?

Day 46 · The One Thing

What do you love bringing into a room, even when nobody asked you to? When did you first notice it in yourself?

Day 47 · On the Way

Was there a season you'd have called wasted time while you were in it? A job, a long wait, a stretch where nothing moved. What was it like? What do you see when you look back?

Day 48 · Walk With Him Into It

When's a time you stepped into something and couldn't control how it would end? What did it cost you to go? (The 2019 Texas Adventure transcript has a Pulley telling about leaving roofing and driving a U-Haul north to Wisconsin. If that's yours, it fits here.)

Week 8

A Passion for Beauty

Day 53 · Tangled

When did soul ties stop sounding dumb to you? What happened the first time you actually prayed through one?

Day 54 · Not the End of the Story

After the affairs, what was the first thing that started turning you around? Where did Dad meet you?

Day 55 · Her Story

Tell about a time you learned something from a woman's past that explained a moment you'd taken personally. What did you do? (Her details can stay private. Just what happened in you.)

Day 56 · Fierce for Her

Tell about a time you watched a man bring strength to a woman or a girl without taking anything from her. What did he do?

Week 9

Band of Brothers

Day 57 · Where's My Buffalo?

When's a time you carried something heavy by yourself that you could have handed to somebody? What was it, and how long did you carry it?

Day 59 · Brother Wounds

Was there a man in your life, a brother, a friend, a teammate, who let you down when it counted? What happened, and what did you tell yourself afterward?

Day 60 · Start With One

When you moved to Wisconsin and had to start over, how did you and Bill actually get going? Who reached out first, and what did those first few times look like?

Day 63 · Who Has Your Back?

Tell about a time you and one of your brothers hurt each other or let each other down. What happened, and how did you find your way back?
